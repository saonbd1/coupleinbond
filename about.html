About Couple in Bond

Welcome to Couple in Bond, a playful relationship-tools site for love scores, thoughtful words, community polls, and small ideas that help people feel closer.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make room for more connection. The site gives couples, friends, daters, and curious visitors a light way to start a conversation without treating a score as a serious measure of love.

Why Couple in Bond?

We believe that love is built through attention, humour, care, and repeated small actions. The calculator uses playful details such as “Meme Compatibility” and “Snack Synergy” to create a result that can start a better question.

The Technology

The calculator runs in the browser. If you choose to connect a wallet, you can mint a playful love result as an NFT on Ink Chain. Blockchain records are public and permanent, so read the Privacy page before you mint.

What we publish

CoupleIn Bond publishes original relationship ideas, love quotes, Valentine’s Day content, and community polls. Start with the blog, browse love quotes, or vote in Community Polls.

A clear promise

Our tools are for entertainment and reflection. They do not diagnose a relationship, predict a future, or replace advice from a qualified professional.
