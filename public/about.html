About Couple in Bond

Make room for more connection.

Couple in Bond is a small space for playful tools, thoughtful words, and small rituals that help people feel closer. We built it for couples, close friends, and anyone who wants to make a little more room for the people who matter.

Modern life can be busy. Between work, messages, plans, and everything else asking for attention, the small moments that keep a bond alive can quietly slip away. Couple in Bond is here to bring some of those moments back—a lighthearted score, a question worth asking, a line that says what you feel, or a keepsake that marks a memory in your own way.

We do not believe love needs to be measured, proved, or turned into a test. Our tools are meant to create a smile, open a conversation, and make connection feel lighter—not to define your relationship or tell you how it should look.

Just for fun

Love Calculator

Our Love Calculator turns two names into a playful connection score. Enter your name and your partner’s name, reveal a lighthearted result, and share a smile together.

It is not a scientific test of love or compatibility. It is a small, fun way to create a moment, start a conversation, and celebrate the people who matter to you.

A playful result. A real moment together.

Playful tool

Compatibility Checker

Our Compatibility Checker is a playful way to explore how two people might click. Enter two names, reveal a lighthearted connection score, and let the result become a reason to talk, laugh, or learn something new about each other.

No quiz can capture trust, effort, humor, patience, or shared history. Compatibility is not a formula—and your score is not a judgment. Think of it as a conversation starter for date nights, anniversaries, long-distance check-ins, or a quiet moment together.

The best part is not the number. It is the conversation that follows.

Keep the moment, your way

For people who want to preserve a playful result, Couple in Bond offers an optional on-chain keepsake. After using the Love Calculator, you can connect your wallet and mint an NFT on Ink Chain that records the two entered names and the connection score from that moment.

The NFT is a keepsake for the moment—not proof of compatibility or a relationship status. Wallet connection, transaction approval, and any network fees always remain in your control.
