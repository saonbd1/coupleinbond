A few ways to begin

Tools, conversations, and little keepsakes for a lighter kind of closeness.

PLAYFUL TOOL2 min read

Use our Love Calculator to find your connection score.

Enter two names, reveal a lighthearted result, and keep the moment on Ink Chain if you wish. After you calculate, connect MetaMask and select “Mint Love NFT” in the result window. Your wallet approves a transaction that sends the two entered names and the score to the Couple in Bond contract on Ink Chain. Network fees may apply. The NFT is a keepsake for the moment—not proof of compatibility.
