Privacy Policy

At Couple in Bond, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Love Calculator.

1. Data Collection

We do not store the names you enter in the Love Calculator on our servers. The calculation is performed locally in your browser using a deterministic algorithm based on the names provided.

2. Blockchain Interactions

If you choose to mint your love score as an NFT:

  • Your wallet address and the names entered will be recorded on the Ink Chain blockchain.
  • Blockchain transactions are public by nature and cannot be deleted once confirmed.
3. Cookies

We may use local storage to remember your wallet connection status for a better user experience. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics that identify you personally.

4. Security

We implement standard security measures to protect the integrity of our site. However, as with any online service, we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
