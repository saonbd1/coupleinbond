<h2>Build the foundation before the furniture</h2>
<p>New couples love decorating the relationship: trips, photos, milestones. But the structure underneath matters more than anything displayed on top. Honest communication lets you survive the first real disagreement. Shared values keep small choices from becoming big fights. Patience with flaws turns quirks from red flags into love stories. And fun keeps the whole project worth doing. The poll below asks which foundation you would build first. There is no wrong answer, but there is a wrong order: decoration before structure always cracks later.</p>
<div class="article-callout"><strong>A gentle rule:</strong> say the uncomfortable thing early and kindly. Small honesty now prevents big resentment later.</div>
<h2>Four foundations to lay in year one</h2>
<ul><li>Practice honest communication about needs, not just feelings</li><li>Name your non-negotiable values out loud before they collide</li><li>Give flaws a generous interpretation while they are still small</li><li>Protect fun deliberately: one adventure a month, minimum</li></ul>
