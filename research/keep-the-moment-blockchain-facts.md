## Keep the Moment blockchain facts

### Public facts used for the card

Ink describes itself as an Ethereum OP Stack Layer 2 and part of the Optimism Superchain. Its official documentation highlights EVM compatibility, one-second block times, and fees that are a fraction of Ethereum mainnet fees. Sources: [About Ink](https://docs.inkonchain.com/general/about) and [Ink FAQ](https://docs.inkonchain.com/faq).

Base describes itself as a blockchain built by Coinbase and built on Ethereum. Its official site highlights low-cost transactions, sub-second transactions, and interoperability across chains and ecosystems. The Base fee documentation explains that each transaction has an L2 execution fee and an L1 security fee, and that fee levels change with network activity. Sources: [Base](https://base.org/) and [Base network fees](https://docs.base.org/base-chain/network-information/network-fees).

### Copy decisions

The card presents Ink as the current minting home because the existing CoupleIn Bond mint flow targets Ink Chain. It presents Base as a broader ecosystem and expansion path, without claiming that the current contract already mints on Base.

The card tells visitors that minting writes the score to a blockchain and that a small gas fee is required or can be required before the wallet confirms the transaction. It does not promise a fixed fee because network fees vary with transaction demand and the fee model of the selected chain.
