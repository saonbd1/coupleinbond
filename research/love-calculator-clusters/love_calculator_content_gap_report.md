# CoupleIn Bond Love Calculator Content-Gap Report

## Research scope

This report covers the worldwide English-language opportunity around love calculators, love percentage calculators, relationship compatibility tests, love quotes, and supporting relationship content. The research uses public search results, public pages, and the worldwide Google Trends interface. No paid SEO export is connected.

Public keyword volume is not available from the sources used in this pass. The workbook labels those fields as unavailable. The priority model therefore uses search intent, public SERP recurrence, trend signals, relevance to CoupleIn Bond, and build effort.

## Executive summary

CoupleIn Bond has a useful product advantage: it already has a working name-based calculator, wallet connection, Ink Chain support, and an NFT keepsake path. The site has a stronger product surface than its current supporting content.

The main gap is **explanation around the tool**. Large public calculator pages target the broad “love calculator” query, then answer questions about accuracy, formulas, score meaning, and limitations [1] [2]. Relationship organisations use a different but valuable frame: compatibility is something couples explore through values, communication, effort, and shared growth [3]. CoupleIn Bond can combine both patterns without making scientific or relationship claims that the tool cannot support.

## Trend signals

The worldwide Google Trends comparison showed rising related queries for “love calculator by name” (+200%), “love calculator by age” (+250%), “love calculator Poki” (+750%), and “love percentage calculator by name” (+60%) [4]. These are normalized relative signals, not monthly volumes. The most useful product-relevant signal is the rise of name-based variants. “Love calculator Poki” shows game-platform intent, but it is a secondary harvest opportunity rather than the main brand angle.

The relationship compatibility comparison returned zodiac-related queries as top related terms [4]. This suggests a possible future expansion for date-of-birth or zodiac compatibility. It does not justify changing the current name-based tool until the core page is stronger.

## Content gaps

### Gap 1: Calculator explanation depth

The target has the tool but does not yet explain the score in enough detail. Add sections that answer what the calculator does, how the playful result works, what the percentage means, and why the score is not scientific evidence.

### Gap 2: Trust and privacy language

Public calculator pages use an explicit entertainment disclaimer [1]. CoupleIn Bond can improve trust further by stating that names and results stay in the browser and by linking to the Privacy page. The page can also explain that an optional NFT mint creates a public blockchain record.

### Gap 3: Compatibility reflection

Relate’s compatibility quiz focuses on values, communication, effort, and growth [3]. CoupleIn Bond can use the same themes as reflection prompts after the score. This creates information gain without copying the quiz or presenting a diagnostic result.

### Gap 4: Seasonal landing content

The Valentine’s Day page can target “Valentine’s Day ideas for couples,” “Valentine’s Day date ideas at home,” and “Valentine’s Day ideas for long-distance couples.” It should link to existing articles, polls, quotes, and the calculator.

### Gap 5: Shareable words

The Quotes page can target “love quotes for him,” “love quotes for her,” “short love quotes,” and “romantic quotes for couples.” The page should use original CoupleIn Bond editorial lines or clearly attributed public-domain material. It must not imply that placeholder copy is user-generated content.

## Buyer-journey map

| Stage | User need | Current gap | Page action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Find a fun love or compatibility tool | Weak explanatory support | Improve calculator title, description, and introductory copy |
| Consideration | Decide whether the score is useful | Accuracy and formula questions are not answered | Add FAQ and score-limit sections |
| Engagement | Share or revisit a result | Web3 keepsake is not explained on-page | Explain optional Ink Chain minting in plain English |
| Relationship support | Find a question or activity | Existing content is not linked from the tool | Add links to blog, quotes, polls, and Valentine’s content |

## Recommended publishing order

First, improve the calculator page because it is the core conversion surface. Next, complete the Valentine’s Day and Love Quotes pages because they can capture seasonal and shareable demand. Then improve About and Contact to support trust, navigation, and brand understanding.

## Limitations

The public sources do not provide a reliable worldwide monthly-volume table for these terms. Search volume and keyword difficulty in the workbook are marked as unavailable or directional. Trend values are normalized Google Trends signals. They must not be read as absolute demand.
