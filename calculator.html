What does a love calculator do?

A love calculator by name gives you a playful match score for two people. It is a quick way to start a conversation, share a laugh, or create a small couple ritual.

Couple in Bond adds lighthearted details such as vibe alignment, snack synergy, texting tempo, and meme compatibility. These details are for entertainment. They do not predict whether a relationship will work.

Use it for a fun moment

Try your name and your partner’s name. You can also test a crush, a friend, or two fictional names.

Turn the result into a question

Ask what the score made you think about. Then choose one topic to discuss together.

Ink Chain keepsake

Mint your playful score

After you calculate, connect MetaMask and select “Mint Love NFT” in the result window. Your wallet approves a transaction that sends the two entered names and the score to the Couple in Bond contract on Ink Chain. Network fees may apply. The NFT is a keepsake for the moment—not proof of compatibility.

Love calculator questions

Before you trust the score

Is a love calculator accurate?

A name-based love calculator is for entertainment and reflection. It does not measure the future of a relationship.

How does the Couple in Bond love calculator work?

Enter two names to create a playful compatibility score and lighthearted match details. The result is not a scientific test.

What does a love percentage mean?

It is a playful result for the two names you enter. Use it as a conversation starter, not as proof of compatibility.

Can I use the love calculator for a crush?

Yes. You can use it with a partner, crush, friend, or any two names for a lighthearted result.

Does Couple in Bond store the names I enter?

The calculator runs in your browser. This page does not store the names or result on its own server.

How can I mint my love score on Ink Chain?

Calculate a score, connect MetaMask on Ink Chain, and select Mint Love NFT in the result window. Review and approve the wallet transaction; network fees may apply.
