Galentine’s Day Ideas for Friendship and Self-Love

Galentine’s Day is a chance to celebrate friendship, chosen family, and the people who make ordinary weeks feel more possible.

Published August 13, 2026·By Couple in Bond Editorial

Galentine’s Day is commonly observed on February 13, the day before Valentine’s Day. It can be a brunch, a group call, a care package, or a quiet note to someone who has helped you through a difficult season. The best plan is the one that makes friendship easier to feel.

Plan a low-pressure gathering

Invite friends to bring one food, one story, and one song. Keep the dress code comfortable. You can host brunch, order something together, or meet at a park. The point is not to stage a perfect event; it is to give people a reason to be in the same room.

Send a “you made a difference” note

Write to someone and name the specific way they showed up for you. Maybe they made you laugh, gave you a place to be honest, or remembered what you needed when you did not have the words. Specific gratitude is a gift that does not need a shopping bag.

Self-love can be social: make a plan that protects your energy. Rest, a favorite meal, a walk, and an early night all count as a real celebration.

More Galentine’s Day ideas

  • Build a shared playlist called “Songs that got us here.”
  • Exchange small care packages with a clear spending limit.
  • Host a comfort-film night with a group chat open for commentary.
  • Make a friendship time capsule and choose a date to open it.
  • Ask each person to share one hope for the next season.
  • Give everyone a blank card to write a note to their future self.

Love is bigger than romance. Celebrating friendship gives the word more room, and that is something worth keeping after the seasonal decorations come down.
